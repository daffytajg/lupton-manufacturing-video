# "BUILT" — voiceover script for Joe

~32 seconds. Read it like you're making a point across a table, not reading a brochure.
Confident, a little clipped. The four process words are hard and flat — almost punched.
Slow down and open up on the last three lines.

Record in **two takes** (not line by line — separate recordings spliced together read as choppy).
Any quiet room, phone voice memo at arm's length is fine. Don't edit the pauses out; I'll cut them.

---

## TAKE 1 — the process (≈7 seconds)

> Every part you buy starts as a print.
>
> Cut.
>
> Formed.
>
> Welded.
>
> Finished.

*Beat of silence between each of the four words — about a second. They land on the cuts.*

---

## TAKE 2 — the problem and the close (≈22 seconds)

> Most buyers run that across four shops. Four purchase orders. Four people to call when the holes don't line up.
>
> We do it under one roof.
>
> One print. One project manager. One shop that owns the fit.
>
> Send us the print. Let's build it together. Luptons dot com.

*"Four shops / four purchase orders / four people" — let the frustration build across the three.
Then drop the energy and slow down on "We do it under one roof." That's the turn.
"Luptons dot com" — say it as three words, unhurried.*
