The post

Jobs never sold a 5GB hard drive. He sold a thousand songs in your pocket.

Same product. Different sentence.

Manufacturing is full of 5GB hard drives. Progressive die, 0.005 tolerance, 12-week lead time, ISO 9001. All true. All forgettable.

The engineer reading that quote isn't buying a stamping. They're buying the assembly that ships on time. The line that doesn't go down. The Friday they don't spend on the phone.

Every spec on the quote is real. None of it is the reason they'll call back.

The suppliers that win learned to say what the part does for the customer, not just what the part is.

A thousand songs in your pocket. That's the whole pitch.

What does your product actually do for the person who buys it?
