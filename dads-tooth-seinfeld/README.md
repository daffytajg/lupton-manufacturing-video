# The Tooth — 90s sitcom parody

A short gag video: Dad, mid-bit, in a 1990s New York coffee-shop booth,
explaining how he came to be down one front tooth.

## The bit

> What is the deal with teeth? Thirty-two of 'em, no instructions.
> One day you're eating bread — bread! The softest food we have —
> and now I got thirty-one. And the dentist blames *me*.

## How it was made

| Stage | Tool | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scene still | Higgsfield, GPT Image 2.5 | Source photo used as an identity reference, 4:3, 2K |
| Voice performance | ElevenLabs, eleven_v3 | Voice "Roger — Laid-Back, Casual, Resonant", inline delivery tags |
| Motion + lip sync | Higgsfield, Wan 2.7 | Scene still as start frame, the ElevenLabs track as the audio reference |

The 4:3 frame, flat key lighting and videotape grain are deliberate — they
place the shot in the multi-camera sitcom era rather than making it look
like a modern clip.

Three voice takes were generated (13.8s, 14.2s, 15.2s). The 13.8s take was
used, since Wan 2.7 caps a clip at 15 seconds and the shortest read left
headroom for the sync.
