Our founder

Why the trident.

Our name and logo pay homage to Mark Dick’s contributions to not only our team but also his commitment to the industry and the United States. The trident symbol is a tribute to Mark’s military service as a Navy Frogman, the predecessor to today’s Navy SEALs. It also represents the power of three, a symbol of Strength, Skill and Value.

Mark Dick, our founder, has not only inspired countless financial advisors across the industry, he has also inspired our team to carry on his principles and his passion for working tirelessly to help clients realize their financial goals.
