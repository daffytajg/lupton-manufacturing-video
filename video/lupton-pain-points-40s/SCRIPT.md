# Lupton Associates — "The RFQ Black Hole" (40-second ad)

**Audience:** OEM buyers, sourcing engineers, procurement managers who buy custom
electro-mechanical components (sheet metal, molding, machining, PCBAs, cable assemblies).

**Format:** 16:9, 8 scenes × 5s, cinematic industrial look. VO + music + SFX.

## Research-validated pain points (sources in RESEARCH.md)

1. **The RFQ black hole** — quotes take weeks; suppliers ghost; fewer than 1 in 4
   rapid quotes in custom manufacturing are accurate (Smart Industry via SpecLens).
2. **Messy sourcing process** — email threads, spreadsheets, clarification loops.
3. **Lead times slip** — supply-chain reliability is the #1 sourcing headache.
4. **Offshore quality & communication risk** — opaque subcontracting, defect disputes.
5. **Tariff volatility** — 72% of trade pros call US tariff volatility the most
   impactful 2026 regulatory change; 82% of supply-chain leaders affected (McKinsey/Ivalua).
6. **Single-source risk** — a key supplier going dark can stop your line.

## Voiceover (~88 words ≈ 37s at ad pace)

> Three weeks ago, you sent the RFQ.
> Still... nothing.
> When the quote finally lands — it's wrong. Fewer than one in four are.
> Then the lead time slips. Again.
> And overnight, tariffs rewrite your landed cost.
> Sound familiar?
> There's a faster way to buy custom parts.
> One call to Lupton Associates puts twenty-five vetted manufacturers on your team.
> Sheet metal. Molding. Machining. Electronics. Domestic and overseas.
> Real engineers who answer the phone. Quotes that hold.
> Lupton Associates. Servicing the working world since nineteen sixty-nine.
