## Narration

1. Since 1969, Lupton Associates has helped OEMs source custom manufactured parts.
2. Here's how it works.
3. You place orders directly with the factory. Pricing comes directly from the factory. No reseller layer. No markup.
4. You get an in-house program manager who knows your parts, your drawings, and your timing.
5. Our team sells for a group of manufacturers, so each shop spends less time finding new work and more time building yours. That's why our model has to be as competitive, or more, to win and keep your business.
6. Molding. Casting. Machining. Stamping. Fabrication. Electronic and cable assembly. Prototyping.
7. Our job is to understand what you need, then match it to a shop with the capacity and the capability to run it.
8. When internal capacity runs short, or a new program needs a supplier you don't have yet, we already know who can run it.
9. Send us the drawing, the material, the annual volume, and the timing. We'll come back with a fit and a feasibility estimate.
10. Lupton Associates. Servicing the working world since 1969. luptons.com.
