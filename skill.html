Retirement income, specifically

After the paychecks stop, the math changes.

Generating the income needed in retirement takes a different investment approach than accumulating and growing wealth. A risk-managed strategy can help balance the risk of loss with the need to keep assets invested in order to outpace inflation.

  • Sequence-of-returns risk
    A bad market early in retirement hurts more than the same market later. Withdrawal strategy and hedging are built around that.
  • Income sequencing
    Which accounts to draw from, in what order, and how Social Security timing fits — coordinated with your tax picture.
  • An RICP® on the team
    Peter Noto holds the Retirement Income Certified Professional® designation — advanced training in exactly this phase.
