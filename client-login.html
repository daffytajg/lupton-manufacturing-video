Stay safe

Three habits that stop most fraud.

01

We will never ask for your password.

No one from Trident, &Partners or Fidelity will ever call, text or email asking for your portal password or a one-time code.

02

Verify money movement by voice.

Urgent requests to wire funds — even ones that look like they come from us or a family member — get a call back to a number you already know.

03

Type the address yourself.

Reach the portals from this page or by typing the address, not from links in unexpected emails. Enable two-factor authentication where offered.
